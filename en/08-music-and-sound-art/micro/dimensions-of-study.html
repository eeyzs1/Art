MICRO / 08 · Dimensions of Study

Aural Analysis, Compartment by Compartment

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Dimensions of Study
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Rhythm, melody, harmony, and musical form are distinct layers of analysis; listening in compartments is the method that turns a confused soundscape into an object that can be discussed.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Music and Sound Art; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Interval: The color of distance: consonance and dissonance are relative; tension and release are what drive the music.
  • Polyphony: A rule system in which the voices converse as equals: the fugue is the game of taking those rules to their extreme.
  • Ethnomusicology: Putting "music theory" in relative view: the tunings, notations, and ways of training of different traditions cannot be applied to one another.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Choose 30 seconds of music; listen three times — once only for rhythm, once only for melody, once only for the bass line — and draw a timeline for each.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The three lines can be aligned on one time axis.
