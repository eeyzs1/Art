# Art Study Room (English Edition)

A course for building art-humanities literacy: start from observing, listening and reading, and train comparison, background research, creation and independent judgment step by step. This is the English edition; the Chinese original is at [../index.html](../index.html).

## Open the course

Double-click [index.html](./index.html) — no dependencies needed. Start with the [five-stage roadmap](./reference/roadmap.html), or go straight to [the first fundamentals lesson](./00-art-fundamentals/lessons/01.html). Every page header carries a language toggle linking to the corresponding page of the Chinese edition.

## Accurate answers to two questions

**The previous version did not list every category.** It held only the main-field map. The course now expands into 14 teaching units and 342 index entries covering materials, media, genres, professional divisions and theory fields; they cross one another and must not be read as 342 mutually exclusive categories. No fixed catalog can exhaust all the world's art traditions and new practices. This page is a broad study index, not a closed totality or the current official discipline catalog.

**Category courses from beginner to independent practice and research are written; full specialty tutorials for every subfield are not exhausted.** 126 lessons in total, nine per category; every stage has concrete text, examples, assignments and feedback criteria; each taxonomy group adds a 15-minute micro-lesson (33 in total). Lesson 09 is masterwork study and a long-term project — it promises no mastery from reading alone. The [coverage chart](./reference/coverage.html) states item by item: subfields use the shared category course and the group micro-lessons; dedicated full specialty courses have not been opened separately. Index entries, generated page counts and project templates are not passed off as complete expertise.

**The division of labor between method and knowledge.** The lesson texts train method; the [core glossary](./reference/glossary.html) (one-sentence definitions of 132 terms), the [anchor timeline](./reference/timeline.html) (collection entries for 127 real works, China and other traditions side by side) and the 33 group micro-lessons provide the content layer, and every lesson 09 has concrete study starting points. They do not form a complete art-history narrative; for the systematic study of history, pair them with a general survey of world and Chinese art, and verify every statement as the method lessons require.

## Categories need several dimensions

Medium and form, subject and use, region and community, history and style, the institutions of production and dissemination, and theory and professional division of labor are different dimensions. The same work can fall in several places — shadow puppetry, for example, connects form-making, theatre, sound, narrative and folk practice at once. Region, history and style cannot be represented by European periodization alone; every regional tradition needs further subdivision inside itself.

Institutional references: the [MOE 2022 graduate catalog](https://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A22/moe_833/202209/t20220914_660828.html) and the [Art Discipline Committee's reading](https://www.moe.gov.cn/fbh/live/2023/55658/dxjy/202312/t20231219_1095135.html) serve degree administration and are not the single classification of all human art. The [UNESCO intangible-heritage domains](https://ich.unesco.org/en/intangible-heritage-domains-00052) attend to transmitted practices and cannot be equated with all art either. See the [classification notes](./reference/taxonomy.html).

## The expanded index of main subfields

The groups below are representative and may overlap; subjects, techniques, forms and research directions are not mixed into one level of mutually exclusive categories. Regional Chinese opera genres, local music and dance, specific crafts and emerging practices are not enumerated one by one.

### 00 · Art Fundamentals

- **History and Theory**: philosophy of art, aesthetics, art history, historiography of art history, art criticism, iconography, visual culture, comparative art studies.
- **People and Institutions**: sociology of art, psychology of art, anthropology of art, art education, art communication, arts management, cultural policy, art market studies.
- **Collecting and Conservation**: curating, museology, history of collecting, provenance research, artifact conservation, preventive conservation, materials and technical research, digital preservation.

### 01 · Painting and Printmaking

- **Materials and Techniques**: drawing, sketching, pen and ink, ink painting, oil painting, watercolor, gouache, acrylic, egg tempera, pastel, collage, mixed media, mural.
- **Printmaking and Publishing**: woodcut, intaglio, lithography, screenprint, mixed-method prints, digital printmaking, artists' books, illustration, comics, lianhuanhua (serialized picture books).
- **Pictorial Questions**: figure painting, portraiture, landscape, shanshui (Chinese landscape painting), bird-and-flower painting, still life, history painting, abstract painting, the calligraphy-painting relationship.

### 02 · Calligraphy and Seal Carving

- **Script Styles**: seal script, clerical script, regular script, running script, cursive script.
- **Research and Practice**: brushwork, character structure, overall composition, copying after models, assembling characters (jizi), creative practice, stele studies, modelbook studies, calligraphy theory, hard-pen calligraphy, seal carving, seal studies, engraved-character art, lettering art.

### 03 · Sculpture and Installation

- **Forms and Media**: sculpture in the round, relief, openwork carving, clay modeling, wood carving, stone carving, metal sculpture, ceramic sculpture, fiber sculpture, soft sculpture, kinetic sculpture, readymades.
- **Space and Events**: installation, site-specific art, light installation, sound installation, public art, land art, monumental art, socially engaged art.

### 04 · Architecture and Landscape

- **Spatial Scales**: architecture, interiors, exhibition spaces, gardens, landscape, urban design, public space, vernacular architecture, heritage conservation and adaptive reuse.
- **Dimensions of Observation**: plan, section, elevation, circulation, structural expression, materials, light environment, acoustic environment, climate response, accessible and inclusive space.

### 05 · Craft and Design

- **Craft Materials**: ceramics, metalwork, jewelry, enamel, lacquer, glass, woodworking, furniture, bamboo and rattan, weaving, dyeing, embroidery, leather, paper crafts, bookbinding.
- **Design Fields**: graphic design, visual communication, typography, book design, information design, brand identity, packaging, product design, industrial design, fashion, textiles, interaction design, service design, experience design, social innovation, speculative design.

### 06 · Photography

- **Genres and Uses**: documentary, photojournalism, street photography, portraiture, landscape, architectural photography, still life, fashion, commercial, conceptual, staged photography, experimental, scientific imaging.
- **Technology and Presentation**: analog photography, digital photography, darkroom, cameraless photography, photobooks, photography archives, photography exhibitions, mobile photography.

### 07 · Literature and Narrative

- **Literary Genres**: poetry, fiction, short stories, novels, essays, personal essays, reportage, memoir, biography, dramatic literature, fables, fairy tales, children's literature, oral literature, web literature.
- **Narrative and Language**: lyric, narrative, point of view, character, structure, rhetoric, meter, translated literature, comparative literature, visual narrative, transmedia storytelling.

### 08 · Music and Sound Art

- **Creation and Practice**: vocal music, instrumental music, ensemble, chorus, conducting, composition, arranging, improvisation, recording and production, electroacoustic music, sound design, sound art, soundscape.
- **Dimensions of Study**: rhythm, meter, melody, interval, mode, harmony, polyphony, musical form, orchestration, ethnomusicology.
- **Traditions and Genres**: Chinese traditional music, Western art music, folk music, religious music, jazz, pop, rock, electronic music, experimental music, regional music traditions.

### 09 · Dance and Body-Based Art

- **Practices and Traditions**: Chinese classical dance, folk and ethnic dance, ballet, modern dance, contemporary dance, street dance, social dance, ritual dance, dance theatre, contact improvisation.
- **Creation and Research**: body and movement, space, time, dynamics, choreography, dance notation, dance history, dance on film and video, dance education.

### 10 · Theatre, Chinese Opera and Live Performance

- **Theatre and the Integrated Stage**: spoken drama, opera, musical theatre, dance drama, puppetry, object theatre, physical theatre, immersive theatre, applied theatre.
- **Xiqu and Quyi**: Peking opera, Kunqu and regional xiqu, crosstalk (xiangsheng), pingshu storytelling, drum songs, tanci and other narrative-singing forms.
- **Live Performance and Collaboration**: acrobatics, magic, circus, performance art, playwriting, directing, acting, stage design, stage lighting, stage sound, costume and makeup, stage management.

### 11 · Film, Animation and Audiovisual Arts

- **Audiovisual Forms**: fiction film, documentary film, experimental film, feature-length film, short film, television drama, online audiovisual media, video art, essay film.
- **Animation**: 2D animation, 3D animation, stop motion, paper-cut animation, sand animation, experimental animation, moving image.
- **Production and Research**: screenwriting, storyboarding, cinematography, acting, editing, sound, score, production design, color grading, visual effects, producing, film history, genre studies, moving-image preservation.

### 12 · Digital Media and Game Art

- **Interaction and Computation**: interactive installation, generative art, algorithmic art, creative coding, data art, net art, software art, robotic art, AI art.
- **Games and Immersion**: video games, tabletop games, independent games, serious games, game narrative, level design, game audiovisuals, virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality, immersive experience.

### 13 · Folk Art and Intermedia

- **Intermedia Practices**: performance art, conceptual art, ecological art, social practice, participatory art, cross-cultural collaboration, food art, olfactory art, bio art, cross-sensory practice.

## How to study

1. Lessons 01–03 of the fundamentals unit build the habit of evidence first; lessons 01–03 of each category take about 15–25 minutes each.
2. Lessons 04–05 are controlled exercises, about 45–90 minutes each; lessons 06–07 are analysis and iteration, about 2–4 hours each. These are suggestions and may be split up.
3. Lesson 08 completes a themed project; lesson 09 is masterwork study and an independent project, needing weeks to months, advanced by outcomes and feedback.
4. The humanities track surveys broadly first, then takes three fields deep; the specialization track picks one subfield for repeated projects, adding the corresponding systematic history and professional technique.
5. Recite from memory on day two; redo with the original work on day seven; keep the drafts, feedback, revisions and unresolved questions of every project round.

Professional physical techniques, instrumental training, material crafts and engineering need the corresponding facilities and in-person guidance. This course emphasizes general literacy, observational analysis, low-cost creation and research method; it is not a replacement for professional qualification training.

## Files and rebuilding

- `en/index.html`: the English edition's main entry; each category's `index.html` and `lessons/01.html` to `09.html`: the guides and lessons.
- `en/reference/`: the method card, five-stage roadmap, core concepts self-check list, core glossary, anchor timeline, category index and coverage chart.
- `en/course-manifest.json`: the reviewable registration of courses and categories.
- `build_course_en.py`: data and template library for the English introductory stage (lessons 01–03); `course_extension_data_en.py`: hand-written data for the expanded categories and the 84 extended lessons; `knowledge_data_en.py`: data for the glossary, timeline anchors and lesson-09 study starting points; `microlesson_data_en.py`: data for the 33 group micro-lessons; `extend_course_en.py`: generator of the English edition under `en/`.
- **Run `python extend_course.py` to rebuild the complete site in both languages (generated pages are overwritten — back up manual edits first).** Running `build_course_en.py` directly is refused, to avoid generating an incomplete intermediate state.
- `check_course.py`: development check script, needs Python Playwright; not needed for study.
- `RESOURCES.md`, `MISSION.md`, `NOTES.md` and `_research/teach/` (Chinese, at the project root): sources, goals, preferences and the teaching-skill snapshot of the original project.

Text, original graphics and local demos work offline; external collections need a connection. Notes and ticks are saved by the browser's localStorage on a best-effort basis; the file protocol and cache clearing may affect retention — save important content elsewhere. The pages do not automatically grade open assignments and do not automatically prove mastery. No learning record is written before the user has completed anything.
