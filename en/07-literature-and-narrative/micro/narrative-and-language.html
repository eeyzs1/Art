MICRO / 07 · Narrative and Language

The Movable Parts of Narrative

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Narrative and Language
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Point of view, time, and structure are parts that can be taken out and tested separately; change one, and all the rest carry the load differently.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Literature and Narrative; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Point of view: The allocation of information rights: who knows what, and when, is the mechanism of all suspense.
  • Rhythm: The design of time in the layer of sound: why does this sentence read fast? Sentence length and punctuation are governing the breath.
  • Literature in translation: Every choice the translator makes is an interpretation: to compare two translations is to compare two readings.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Take a 300-word narrative, rewrite it changing only the point of view, and list the gains and losses of information.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

Each gain or loss is matched to the change of viewpoint.
