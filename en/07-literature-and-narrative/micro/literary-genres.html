MICRO / 07 · Literary Genres

Genre Is a Frame of Expectation

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Literary Genres
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Readers enter a text with generic expectations: the "once upon a time" of the fairy tale, the source-pressure of reportage, the serial rhythm of web fiction. Genre is a contract, and it can also take effect by being broken.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Literature and Narrative; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Memoir and biography: The slant of first-person memory against the archival duty of the third person: two ways of being honest.
  • Reportage: Literary technique plus verifiability: once the checking is lost, only sentiment is left.
  • Serialization: The end-of-chapter hook shapes the structure: the rhythm of waiting itself is part of the form.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Rewrite a 200-word prose passage as the opening of a fairy tale and as the opening of reportage, keeping the core of fact intact in both.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The layer of fact is identical in the two versions.
