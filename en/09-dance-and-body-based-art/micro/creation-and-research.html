MICRO / 09 · Creation and Research

Choreography Organizes Variables

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Creation and Research
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Body, space, time, and effort are the four faders of choreography; a good work is not every fader wide open, but clear relations within limited parameters.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Dance and Body-Based Art; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Effort (dynamics): Effort: the same movement with a different energy is a different syntax — light and heavy are language, not decoration.
  • Dance notation: The cost of turning movement into preservable notation: what is written down, and what is lost.
  • Dance film/video: Choreography of the camera: camera position and editing become part of the body.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Make three versions of an eight-count phrase changing only "effort," and ask viewers to tell the versions apart and rank them.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The differences among the three versions are recognized reliably by others.
