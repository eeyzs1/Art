MICRO / 11 · Animation

A Frame-by-Frame Worldview

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Animation
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Animation is not "pictures that move": it constructs time frame by frame, and physical law is a choice rather than a default.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Film, Animation and Audiovisual Arts; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Stop motion: The tactile time of materials: every twenty-fourth of a second must be set by hand.
  • Paper-cut animation: Flatness joined with the fine-arts tradition: the path of the Shanghai Animation Film Studio repays study.
  • Relations between frames: Anticipation, action, follow-through: the illusion of movement comes from the design of relations between frame and frame.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Make a 12-frame flip-book animation that shows only the three-part rhythm of "anticipation — action — follow-through"; do not try to draw well.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

Viewers can recognize the three-part rhythm.
