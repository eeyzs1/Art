MICRO / 11 · Production and Research

Departmental Languages and Overall Judgment

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Production and Research
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Every department has its own grammar: the cut point, the grading range, the layers of the mix; the director's work is to make trade-offs among departmental languages.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Film, Animation and Audiovisual Arts; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Storyboard: Previsualization — and when to set the storyboard aside and leave room for discovery on the spot.
  • Color grading: The chemistry of mood: its fit with the art and lighting departments decides the logic of the overall palette.
  • Film preservation: Prints and versions: of which year's restoration is the "old film" you are watching?

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Choose one scene and analyze only where the cut points fall in the action (during the action / after the action); write down three regularities.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The regularities carry time points and can be tested against counterexamples.
