Art Fundamentals

Observe first, then interpret. The nine lessons cover five stages: begin with the introductory lessons, then controlled exercises, a themed project and masterwork study. Advancement is judged by outcomes and feedback, not by click counts.

Group micro-lessons

Each taxonomy group has a micro-lesson of about 15 minutes connecting its terms to concrete methods and works; notes are saved on this page.

  • Theory Is a Toolbox for Asking Questions (History and Theory, about 15 minutes)
  • Who Pays, Who Watches, Who Defines (People and Institutions, about 15 minutes)

The taxonomy index for this category

History and Theory

philosophy of art, aesthetics, art history, historiography of art history, art criticism, iconography, visual culture, comparative art studies

People and Institutions

sociology of art, psychology of art, anthropology of art, art education, art communication, arts management, cultural policy, art market studies

Collecting and Conservation

curating, museology, history of collecting, provenance research, artifact conservation, preventive conservation, materials and technical research, digital preservation

These entries come from different dimensions — medium, form, use and research — and may overlap. They share the method training of the nine lessons above, with group micro-lessons as entry points; dedicated full specialty courses have not been written for every single term.

Choosing your own direction

After lesson 05, choose a subfield with a well-defined object in the index and focus the assignments of lessons 06–09 on the same direction. Each round should retain original-work analysis, your own attempts, external feedback and substantive revision. Where specialized physical techniques, material crafts or engineering are involved, the corresponding facilities and in-person guidance are required; this text emphasizes art fundamentals, analysis, low-cost creation and research.
