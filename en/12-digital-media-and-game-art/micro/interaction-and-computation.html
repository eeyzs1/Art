MICRO / 12 · Interaction and Computation

Rules, Data, and Emergence

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Interaction and Computation
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

The distinctive trait of computational media: behavior is produced by rules in real time; the work is a system, not a fixed object.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Digital Media and Game Art; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Generative art: A rule system produces the outcome, with randomness only an optional ingredient: a system without randomness can still be generative art; selection and combination are authorial acts, so keep a process archive.
  • Data art: The choice of data is already a stance: what is chosen and what is left out are both editing.
  • AI art: The process archive: tools, prompts, batches, selections, and revisions must all be traceable before responsibility can be discussed.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Write an "if... then..." rule system (paper is enough), run it three times, and record which run produced a result your design did not foresee.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

You can tell "designed behavior" from "emergent behavior."
