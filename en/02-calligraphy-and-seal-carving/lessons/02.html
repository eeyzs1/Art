LESSON 02.02

Character Structure and Overall Composition

Lesson 2 of 9 · 15–25 minutes
Goal of this lesson: use the method of "Character Structure and Overall Composition" to add one more evidence-based observation of a work.

01 · Understand one concept

Character structure studies the organization inside a single character; overall composition studies the relations among characters, columns, and empty space across the whole work. Seal, clerical, regular, running, and cursive are the customary script categories, and their development was no straight line in which each old style was wholly replaced by a new one. Identification can begin with features: seal script is even and elongated; clerical script carries the wavelike flaring stroke ("silkworm head, swallow tail"); regular script is upright and square; running script links its strokes; cursive script abbreviates into signs.

Reading anchor: The Palace Museum: Zhao Mengfu, Luoshen fu in running script. Look together with the museum's notes on character structure, strokes, and layout. The everyday examples and exercises below are an original teaching design, not that institution's own text or authoritative conclusions.

02 · Study one example

With the Palace Museum's material on Zhao Mengfu's running script, look first at the distance between characters, then at the balance of each column; you need not identify every character before you begin to look.

Return to the lab from lesson one: can the concept of this lesson explain one of the changes you saw there? State the difference between evidence and inference.

03 · Do one exercise

Arrange four characters in a loose version and a dense version, and explain which version slows the pace of viewing.

Open after finishing: check your approach

Check three things: whether you completed the assigned comparison; whether you pointed to specific shapes, movements, words, or sounds; whether you kept facts and interpretation apart. Your answer need not match the impressions of the example, as long as you can give grounds.

Check especially in this lesson: Overall composition looks at the organization of the whole; character structure looks at the single character.
