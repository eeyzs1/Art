MICRO / 06 · Genres and Uses

Genre Is a Mode of Use

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Genres and Uses
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

"Documentary" is not a style but a promise about use: caption, source, and shooting conditions constitute its chain of evidence. Staged and experimental work is not false, but it requires a different honesty.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Photography; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Documentary: The chain of evidence: who shot it, when, where, and under what conditions — lose one item and the use changes.
  • Scientific imaging: An aesthetics in the service of measurement: scale bar, light source, and waveband may never be omitted from the labeling.
  • Conceptual photography: Is the photograph a prop for the concept or its subject? The answer decides the standard of evaluation.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Write three captions for the same photograph — documentary, commercial, conceptual — and compare what each promises.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

You can point out what remains "unverified" in each caption.
