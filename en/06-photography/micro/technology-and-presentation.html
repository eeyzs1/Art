MICRO / 06 · Technology and Presentation

The Presentation Chain from Darkroom to Screen

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Technology and Presentation
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

The same negative is three objects — in the darkroom, on the screen, and in the book; the presentation chain is a constituent of the work.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Photography; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Darkroom: Local dodging and burning: to develop is to interpret, and every enlargement is a decision.
  • Photobook: Rhythm designed through sequence and page turn: the temporality of the book is governed by the speed of the reader's hand.
  • Photographic archives: Institutional custody decides visibility: accessioning, cataloging, and digitization each have their own threshold.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Shoot the same scene three times on a phone; adjust the presentation for screen, print, and projection, and record how the "best" one changes with the medium.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The conclusion clearly separates the "file" from the "presentation."
