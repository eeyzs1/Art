How to start without drowning in the numbers

Read lessons 01–03 of the fundamentals unit first, then take any category through lessons 01–05. Each time, keep a concrete artwork observation, a controlled exercise and revision notes; then decide whether to go deeper into lessons 06–09. You need not take every category at once.

The course reads offline and the interactive demos call no external services; institutional works, recordings and external reading need a connection. Open assignments must actually be done by you — the course automatically grades no artistic quality.
