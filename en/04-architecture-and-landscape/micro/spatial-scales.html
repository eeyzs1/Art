MICRO / 04 · Spatial Scales

A Continuous Scale from Room to City

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Spatial Scales
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

The handle at the door, the room, the street, the city: the same bodily experience at different scales; thinking across scales is the shared training of this group.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Architecture and Landscape; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Vernacular architecture: Architecture without architects: the long trial-and-error product of convention, climate, and local materials.
  • Adaptive reuse: Old structure, new use: what to keep and what to alter — each item is a contestable decision.
  • Public space: Who is welcome to stay: seating, shade, and accessibility decide who actually uses the space.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Draw a route you walk often, mark three points where the scale breaks (e.g., from indoors to an alley mouth to a square), and record how your bodily sense changes.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

Each break point carries one observable piece of evidence.
