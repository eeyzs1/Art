MICRO / 04 · Dimensions of Observation

Learning the Vocabulary for Reading Drawings

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Dimensions of Observation
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Plan, section, and elevation are three ways of cutting, each with places it cannot reach; circulation, light, sound, and climate are evidence from the bodily dimension.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Architecture and Landscape; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Section: Height relations the plan cannot show: the difference in light distribution between a clerestory and a side window is seen in section.
  • Acoustic environment: Surface materials decide reverberation: hard walls, soft upholstery, and an acoustical ceiling are three sound solutions.
  • Climate response: Deep eaves and orientation are at once form and strategy: a history of climate is stored in form.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Find a plan of a room (or draw one), add a section, and annotate the direction of light and one acoustic feature.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The drawing and on-site observation can be checked against each other.
