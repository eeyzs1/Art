# Mission: Art and humanities literacy

## Why
Study the main branches of the arts to build art-humanities literacy, so that everyday looking, reading, listening, and performance-going become experience that can be analyzed and discussed.

## Success looks like
- Offer three concrete observations on an unfamiliar work, distinguishing fact, interpretation, and evaluation.
- Understand the main categories and their sub-branches and choose a direction for deeper study.
- Write a 180-word review supported by verifiable background and acknowledge gaps in the material.

## Constraints
- English edition, translated from the Chinese original; HTML-first.
- Designed for complete beginners for now; the schedule is a suggestion and has not been fixed by the user.

## Out of scope
The user has not excluded any field; this delivery is an introductory survey and presumes no need for professional examination preparation or vocational training.
