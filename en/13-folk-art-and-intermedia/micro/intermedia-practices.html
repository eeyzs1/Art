MICRO / 13 · Intermedia Practices

The Boundary Is the Material

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Intermedia Practices
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

These practices take the boundary between media as their material: edible, smellable, to be lived with; to criticize them one returns to rules and experience, instead of asking first "is this art."

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Folk Art and Intermedia; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Conceptual art: The division of labor between execution and idea: the copyright and the re-execution of instruction works are real problems.
  • Bio art: The ethics and the duty of care for living systems: if the work is alive, the author owes it care.
  • Cross-sensory practice: Synesthesia is not spectacle: how multiple sensory channels calibrate one another is the real question.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Design a one-minute "single sense" experience (smell only, or touch only); write the rules, the expected difficulties, and an exit mechanism.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The rules restrict the mode of perception and provide for the participant's right to withdraw.
