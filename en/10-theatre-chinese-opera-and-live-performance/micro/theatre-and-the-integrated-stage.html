MICRO / 10 · Theatre and the Integrated Stage

The Leading Medium of the Integrated Stage

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Theatre and the Integrated Stage
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

Opera is led by music, dance drama by the body, puppetry by the object; "integrated" is not the adding of elements but the choice of a leading medium.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Theatre, Chinese Opera and Live Performance; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Puppetry: The soul of the object: whether the puppeteer's rhythm of breath is visible decides the puppet's sense of life.
  • Immersive theatre: The audience becomes a variable of space: the freedom to move is both a gift and a burden.
  • Applied theatre: Theater tools applied in educational and community settings: the goal has changed, so the standard of evaluation must change as well.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Take the core of one story and write one sentence each for opera, puppetry, and immersive theatre on "how the leading medium tells it."

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

Each of the three proposals points to the distinctive capacity of its leading medium.
