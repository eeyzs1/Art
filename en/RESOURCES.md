# Art study resources

## Knowledge

- [Getty: formal analysis](https://www.getty.edu/education/teachers/building_lessons/formal_analysis.html): See the definitions of line, shape, space, and color with examples from the collection.
- [The Palace Museum: Zhao Mengfu, Luoshen fu in running script](https://intl.dpm.org.cn/Calligraphy_collection/63948.html): Look together with the museum's notes on character structure, strokes, and layout.
- [V&A: collection search](https://www.vam.ac.uk/collections): Search digital design or video games and study the medium alongside the objects.
- [Carnegie Hall: Music Educators Toolbox](https://www.carnegiehall.org/Education/Programs/Music-Educators-Toolbox): Choose Rhythm & Meter or Singing and follow the exercises set by the institution itself.
- [BC curriculum: elements of dance](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/content/ccdance-foundationsgrade-11elements-de-la-danse): Check the observation dimensions of body, space, time, and effort.
- [BFI: reading a short film or scene](https://ugc.futurelearn.com/uploads/files/d3/95/d395220f-bbb0-4e76-810f-e3caca25727b/Reading_a_short_film_or_scene.pdf): Use the BFI question sheet to watch shots, editing, and sound.
- [Met timeline of art history](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/timeline-of-art-history): verify background by region, period, and theme; avoid stringing all of art along a Western style history alone.
- [Ministry of Education guidelines for calligraphy education](https://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A26/s8001/201301/t20130125_147389.html): a reference for studying script styles and the appreciation of steles and modelbooks.

## Wisdom (Communities)

Prefer to discuss one of your own observation records with educators and other visitors in the official education programs of a local public museum, a library reading group, or an introductions session at an established theater. The user's city has not been queried, so no specific events or opening hours are invented; participation is optional.

## Gaps and limits of use

Institutional pages and search summaries were read for this project; not every external link carries every claim of the course. The V&A collections gateway is an entry for further searching and does not serve as item-by-item evidence for narrative theory or game mechanics. Literature, xiqu, games, and folk art currently lean on original observation exercises; deeper study should add specific editions, community self-accounts, and specialized sources. Music, dance, and film each have institutional courses or analytical question sheets; visual formal analysis is not taken as a universal authority for all the arts. All simulations explain relationships only and do not replicate real historical works.

- 08 [Yale Open Courses: Listening to Music](https://online.yale.edu/courses/listening-music): A systematic extension for layered listening and musical form; that course deals mainly with Western music and cannot represent all musical traditions.
- 09 [BC curriculum: elements of dance](https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/content/ccdance-foundationsgrade-11elements-de-la-danse): Check the observation dimensions of body, space, time, and effort.
- 10 [V&A: theatre and performance collection](https://www.vam.ac.uk/collections/theatre-performance): Browse performance, stage design and archives; specialized xiqu technique requires practitioner resources for the specific genre.
- 11 [BFI: reading a short film or scene](https://ugc.futurelearn.com/uploads/files/d3/95/d395220f-bbb0-4e76-810f-e3caca25727b/Reading_a_short_film_or_scene.pdf): Use the BFI question sheet to watch shots, editing, and sound.
- 12 [V&A: on the design of a videogames exhibition](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/about-videogames-exhibition): Observe game design and participation questions from an institutional curatorial perspective; the systematic exercises here are not that institution's assignments.
- 13 [UNESCO: intangible heritage domains](https://ich.unesco.org/en/intangible-heritage-domains-00052): Understand how living practices cut across many domains; intangible heritage is not equivalent to all art, nor is it made only of collectible objects.

- [Met departments and conservation research](https://www.metmuseum.org/departments): the professional division of labor among conservation, materials research and curating. The local observation exercises are not treated as a conservation-technique tutorial.
