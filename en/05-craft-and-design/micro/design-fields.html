MICRO / 05 · Design Fields

Design Is Defining the Problem

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Design Fields
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

What the entries in this group share: situation and task come first, form comes after. Style is a result, not a point of departure.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Craft and Design; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Information design: Testing hierarchy against misreading: whether the key information can be found within five seconds is a hard metric.
  • Service design: The touchpoint map: experience is organized across many points of contact in a process, not only in the moment of use.
  • Speculative design: Asking through "what if": it does not seek implementation; it seeks to open a space for discussion.
  • Fashion and textiles: Design tasks fitted to the body: cut pattern, fabric, and how a garment goes on and off jointly decide function; "fashion" is only one layer of it.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Write a testable design brief: who, in what situation, performs what action, and how success is judged.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The success criteria are observable and do not depend on adjectives.
