Expanded Index of Art Categories

First, an answer: did the previous version list everything?

No. The previous version covered the main creative fields but omitted many subfields and mixed some distinct dimensions together. This edition expands the index and states its boundaries, without claiming to exhaust every local tradition, new-media practice or their names worldwide.

Classification is a tool for asking questions and retrieving. Medium, function, region, history and object of study each yield different, overlapping catalogs; no single tree is both mutually exclusive and able to exhaust all art.

Do not mix the six dimensions

DimensionExamples and role
Medium / formPainting, sound, the body, space, computation; answers what organizes experience.
Subject / genre / usePortrait, epic, comedy, ritual, advertising; these concepts sit at different levels and need field-specific interpretation.
Region / community traditionDifferent regions of China, Europe, South Asia, Southeast Asia, West Asia and North Africa, sub-Saharan Africa, the Americas, Oceania and diaspora communities; each holds vast internal differences.
History / styleThe names of periods and movements belong within their local histories; European periodization must not be laid over the whole globe.
Production / disseminationCourt, religion, community, market, public institutions, online platforms; one work may pass through several systems.
Theory / professional division of laborAesthetics, history and theory, criticism, education, curating, conservation, management; research fields and creative media are not the same dimension.

Relation to official catalogs

As an institutional reference, China's Ministry of Education 2022 graduate catalog divides professional art degrees into six groups — music, dance, theatre and film, xiqu and quyi, fine arts and calligraphy, and design — and establishes art studies as a discipline. That catalog serves degree administration and is not a classification of all human art. This course takes a broad humanistic view and therefore also includes literature, architecture and landscape as related creative fields.

Sources: the 2022 catalog notice, the Art Discipline Committee's reading. This page describes that version only and does not claim it equals the latest complete set of all subsequent adjustments.

The UNESCO intangible-heritage framework attends to mutually crossing domains — oral expression, performance, social practice — which include many art practices and also knowledge and activities whose primary character is not artistic; the ICH lists therefore cannot replace an art taxonomy. Which border fields exist?

Cooking and food, incense and smell, tea and flowers, body adornment, gardening, ritual, videogames and biological media can become art practices in specific contexts, but not all related activities automatically belong to art. When judging, record the concrete practice, the community's self-description, the context of presentation and the questions posed; do not count whole trades of life wholesale as new major categories.

The same object can carry several labels: shadow puppetry can belong at once to form-making, performance, sound, narrative and folk practice. Its main entry in this course is 13, linked to 10, 08, 07 and 03.
