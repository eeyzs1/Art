Core Concepts Self-Check List

The core concepts and acceptance checks of all 126 lessons, arranged by category and the five stages. How you tick matters more than the list itself.

How to use this list

  1. After finishing a lesson, close the page and recite the concept before coming back to tick; each small line is that lesson's key sentence or acceptance check — the minimal cue for reciting.
  2. If recitation fails the next day, untick it; verify again on day seven with a new work.
  3. Tick lesson 09 only after completing one full project round, receiving feedback and making a revision.
  4. Progress is saved in this browser and lost when the cache is cleared; real evidence of mastery is your work and notes, not the ticks themselves.
