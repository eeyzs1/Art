Taxonomy and Course Coverage Chart

14 units, 126 lessons and 33 group micro-lessons have been written; the table below registers 342 taxonomy entries. The number of entries is not the number of independent art categories, nor the number of specialty courses.

How to read the table

“Shared category course” means the entry can use the foundational methods and project lessons of its category; “group micro-lesson” is the 15-minute dedicated lesson of the entry's group; “no dedicated specialty course yet” means no textbook exists that teaches its full history, techniques, works and professional training. This table does not count an index entry as a completed course.
