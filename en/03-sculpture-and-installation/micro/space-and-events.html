MICRO / 03 · Space and Events

Space and Time Become Materials

Group micro-lesson · about 15 minutes · Space and Events
Goal: turn this group of terms into at least three usable observation points and one exercise.

01 · Why this group of terms exists

These practices turn "where, how long, who takes part" into parameters of the work; documentation and reperformance therefore become central questions.

This lesson belongs to the extension layer of Sculpture and Installation; for method foundations see lessons 01–03 of the category.

02 · Observation points

  • Site-specific art: Made for this place: after relocation, even an exact rebuilding faces an altered web of context.
  • Socially engaged art: The ethical weight of "relations" as medium: participants are not material, and the right to withdraw must genuinely exist.
  • Land art: Inaccessibility challenges the exhibition system: most people reach the work only through photographs and maps.

For term definitions see the core glossary; for entries to real artworks see the anchor timeline.

03 · Do one exercise

Design a "one-minute event" for a corner you know well: write the rules, the audience position, and how it ends; do not stage it.

04 · Acceptance check

Expand after finishing: check your reasoning

The rules are executable, and the audience role and the way out are explicit.
