These entries come from different dimensions — medium, form, use and research — and may overlap. They share the method training of the nine lessons above, with group micro-lessons as entry points; dedicated full specialty courses have not been written for every single term.

Choosing your own direction

After lesson 05, choose a subfield with a well-defined object in the index and focus the assignments of lessons 06–09 on the same direction. Each round should retain original-work analysis, your own attempts, external feedback and substantive revision. Where specialized physical techniques, material crafts or engineering are involved, the corresponding facilities and in-person guidance are required; this text emphasizes art fundamentals, analysis, low-cost creation and research.
